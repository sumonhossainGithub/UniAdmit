Exam Day Must-Haves Checklist

2 Color Print Admit Cards: Clear photograph & barcode on standard A4 offset paper.
Original HSC Registration Card: Or official board document & valid photo identity.
Black Ballpoint Pens (2–3 pcs): Transparent body only. Gel pens and pencils strictly barred for OMR.
Approved Non-Programmable Calculator: Casio fx-100MS, fx-570MS, fx-991MS (only where circular permits).
Transparent Drinking Bottle: Without label or sleeve (if permitted by exam hall).

Universal Exam Regulations

Negative Marking Warning: 0.25 marks will be deducted for each incorrect MCQ answer across all public admission tests.
Hall Reporting: Candidates must occupy their allotted seats at least 30 minutes before test commencement.
Barred Items: Smartphones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, digital calculators & bags are strictly prohibited in the exam hall.
OMR Cautions: Roll number and registration number bubbles must be filled flawlessly. Scratching or fluid use will void the sheet.
