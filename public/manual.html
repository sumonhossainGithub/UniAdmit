PREFACE & SYSTEM INFO OVERVIEW
UniAdmit is an integrated centralized admission management portal engineered to eliminate redundant university application workflows. Students can apply across multiple institutions simultaneously with AI-driven qualification matching.
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Portal URL http://localhost:3000
Architecture Node.js, Express, SQLite3 Engine
Supported Roles Student, University Manager, Admin
Security Bcrypt hash, Role session guard
Compatibility Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Mobile Safari
ℹ️
Notice: This user manual is exclusively designed for prospective university applicants (Student role).
UniAdmit Central Portal PAGE 1
STEP 1: REGISTRATION & LOGIN ACCOUNT
1.1 Account Registration
Navigate to the login portal and click "Register as a Student". Provide your full legal name, active email, mobile number, and a strong password.
1.2 Role Selection & Security
Ensure you select Student on the login switch. The system verifies role credentials; manager accounts cannot access the applicant portal.
Applicant Input Validation Rule
Full Name Must match SSC/HSC certificate
Email Address Used for verification & admit delivery
Phone (MFS) 11-digit SMS alert contact
⚠️
Important: Never share your password or credential tokens with third parties.
Authentication Module PAGE 2
STEP 2: PROFILE WIZARD MANDATORY
System Rule: Students cannot apply to any university until profile completion reaches 100%.
Step 2.1: Personal
Parents' names, DOB, nationality, and permanent address.
Step 2.2: SSC Exam
Roll, reg. number, board, passing year & GPA (scale 5.0).
Step 2.3: HSC Exam
Stream (Sci/Arts/Com), roll, board & achieved GPA.
Step 2.4: Uploads
Passport photo, signature scan, and quota declaration.
✓
Once saved, your dashboard unlocks: "Profile Complete (100%)" in active green state.
Student Profile Module PAGE 3
STEP 3: SMART MATCHING AI ENGINE
3.1 Automated Qualification Match
Click "Smart Suggestions". The algorithmic filter reads your SSC GPA, HSC GPA, and study stream to display matching universities.
Evaluation Metric Evaluation Logic
Minimum SSC GPA Checks threshold (e.g. min 3.50)
Minimum HSC GPA Checks threshold (e.g. min 3.50)
Combined GPA SSC GPA + HSC GPA ≥ Required
Discipline Stream Science, Arts/Hum, Commerce
💡
Tip: Ineligible institutions are tagged with requirement breakdowns so you can plan backup applications.
Recommendation Engine PAGE 4
STEP 4 & 5: APPLY & PAYMENT CHECKOUT
4.1 Multi-University Selection
Tick multiple university checkboxes. The system aggregates all departmental application fees dynamically without retyping profile data.
5.1 MFS Payment Gateway
Click "Proceed to Payment". Select bKash, Nagad, or Rocket. Send the required fee to the displayed merchant account.
MFS Channel Merchant Type Required Input
bKash Send Money / Payment 10-digit TrxID
Nagad Merchant Pay 8-10 char TrxID
Rocket 12-digit Bill Pay Txn Reference
Payment Module PAGE 5
STEP 6: STATUS & ADMIT CARD VERIFICATION
Track your submission status in real-time under "My Applications":
1. Pending Review
University administration is verifying your academic credentials and transaction payment ID.
2. Approved & Admit Card
Application validated. Click "Download Admit Card" to obtain your examination slip featuring roll, venue, and QR token.
3. Rejected / Incomplete
Triggered if TrxID is invalid. Contact the university helpdesk immediately to update receipt details.
Admit Card Slip Module PAGE 6
FAQS & TROUBLESHOOTING HELPDESK
Q: Can I apply to universities from different streams?
A: Yes, if the university offers unit migration (e.g. Science students applying to BBA / Arts units).
Q: Why is my application still 'Pending Review'?
A: Verification typically takes 12-24 business hours. Contact helpdesk if pending over 48 hours.
Q: What if I entered the wrong TrxID?
A: Submit a support ticket immediately with your MFS SMS screenshot for manual ledger matching.
