Google Provider Setup

To connect with your real Google account, the Google Provider toggle must be switched ON in your Supabase project settings.

1. Go to Supabase Auth Providers →
2. Click Google and toggle Enable Google provider.
3. Paste Google Client ID & Secret, then click Save.
